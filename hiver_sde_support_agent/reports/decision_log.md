# Decision log

1. **Brand = AppleSupport.** It is a high-volume support account in TWCS and can be isolated through `author_id`.
2. **Customer→agent pairs.** We use `in_response_to_tweet_id` rather than assuming adjacent rows form a conversation.
3. **Keep original text.** Cleaning is limited to normalization inside models so the evidence remains human-readable.
4. **Discover intents before final taxonomy.** K-means + TF-IDF is used to expose recurring themes; the final labels are manually defined from those examples.
5. **Human-labelled training candidates.** Intent labels used for the classifier are not silently generated by the model.
6. **Time-based holdout.** The last 20% of conversations is reserved for evaluation to reduce temporal leakage.
7. **Golden set is untouched.** It is sampled from the held-out period and is never used for training or retrieval.
8. **TF-IDF classifier first.** It is fast, interpretable, cheap, and gives a credible simple ML system.
9. **Historical retrieval.** Brand-specific past resolutions are the evidence source for replies.
10. **LLM generation is constrained.** The LLM receives only retrieved examples and explicit anti-hallucination rules.
11. **Routing is deterministic.** High-risk patterns, low confidence, and weak evidence trigger human review outside the LLM.
12. **No retrieval leakage.** The index is built only from development data; otherwise retrieval metrics would be artificially inflated.
13. **Two trivial/simple baselines.** Majority intent and lexical intent baselines establish whether the classifier adds value.
14. **Judge calibration before reporting.** LLM-as-judge scores are not treated as ground truth until compared with human labels.
15. **Headline-number warning.** Average accuracy can hide rare but consequential failures, so macro-F1 and routing safety are reported separately.
